Support — Snap & Wind

Snap & Wind is a disposable-film camera for iPhone from Wilmoth Engineering, LLC. This page is how to reach us.

You use Snap & Wind without an account and without signing in.

Your photos

Photos are stored on your device. Backup to your own iCloud is optional. Photos go to your Photos library or get shared only when you choose.

If you need us, email hello@wilmoth.engineering and say it is about Snap & Wind.
