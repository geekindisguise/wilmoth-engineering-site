Support — Lodebook

Lodebook is a field notebook for spots, finds, notes, and mining claims on iPhone, iPad, and Mac from Wilmoth Engineering, LLC. This page is how to use Lodebook and how to reach us.

Start with Help in the app.

You use this App Store release without an account and without signing in.

Local or iCloud

Setup and Settings list Local and iCloud.

Local and iCloud are the same records. Local is this device. iCloud is this Apple ID — backup and extra iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Settings → Take it with you writes a zip of this device.

Map

Open the Map tab. The default base layer is Satellite photos (Esri). Settings → Default map also offers Topographic map (USGS).

The Layers sheet lists Topographic map (USGS), Satellite photos (Esri), Photos plus contours (USGS), Terrain shading (Esri), Lidar relief (USGS), and Street map (Apple). Street map is labeled Online only.

Esri and USGS tiles download on Wi-Fi when the device is online, field mode is off, and Low Power Mode is off. Tiles already on the device draw with the radio off.

Spots and finds

On first launch, choose Who are you? → Work → Rock Hounder. The same choice is Settings → Work. A fresh install starts on Rock Hounder. Phone tabs are Map, Spots, Notes, and More.

Add a spot from Spots. The sheet title is New Spot. Name is required. You can also enter county, state, location, a map coordinate, and a note on the spot.

Long-press the map to drop a find at that coordinate. Choose Dig Site, Hazard, or Marker, then a label, mineral, color, notes, and photos.

Notes

The Notes tab is the field log. New Note asks for a type, a label, notes, and photos. Tagging a spot or a mining claim is optional. When the phone has a location fix, the note can use that coordinate.

A mining claim

Settings → Work → Small Miner. The tab title becomes Claims.

On the Claims list, tap Add a claim, then Start a mining claim. You can also long-press the add button and choose Start a mining claim. Name the claim. Type can be Lode, Placer, Mill site, or Tunnel site.

Free includes two new mining claims, mining claims already on this device, and spots, finds, and notes.

Stake Day

Open a mining claim while Work is Small Miner and tap Stake Day. Stake Day is a field aide for corners, monuments, and a GPS walk-in. Phone GPS accuracy is shown. Ground monuments win.

Offline maps

On a spot or a mining claim, open the actions menu and choose Offline map. Download a field kit for that place, or download the current layer. Layers → Download offline is the other way in.

Pack downloads stay on Wi-Fi unless Download maps on cellular is on. Field mode blocks the pack download.

Plus

Starting a third mining claim opens Plus. Plus covers new mining claims beyond two. The price on the sheet comes from the App Store.

With two mining claims already saved, Add a claim and Start a mining claim open Plus and leave your records as they are. Adding a spot stays available.

The Plus sheet includes Restore purchases. Terms of Use is Apple’s standard EULA. Manage or cancel Plus in Settings on your device.

If you still need us, email hello@wilmoth.engineering and say it is about Lodebook.
