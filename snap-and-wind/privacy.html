Privacy Policy — Snap & Wind

Last updated: October 12, 2026.

Snap & Wind is a disposable-film camera for iPhone from Wilmoth Engineering, LLC. This page covers Snap & Wind.

What the app does

You take photos the way you would with a disposable film camera. You use Snap & Wind without an account and without signing in.

Where your photos sit

Photos you take are captured and stored on your device.

Backup to iCloud is optional. When you turn it on, your photos go to your own iCloud account under your Apple ID. That is Apple’s storage, not a Snap & Wind server. We do not operate a server that receives your photos.

Photos are saved to your Photos library or shared only when you choose to do so.

If you delete the app, photos stored only in the app go with it. Copies you saved to Photos or backed up to iCloud stay where you put them.

Tracking and ads

Snap & Wind does not track you and shows no ads. There is no account system.

Camera

Snap & Wind uses camera access only to take photos in the app. It doesn't use your location or microphone.

Analytics

There are no analytics and no third-party SDKs. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Purchases

There's one optional in-app purchase, “Quicker develop”. Apple handles payment entirely, and the app never sees your payment details.

This page on the web

This policy is hosted on wilmoth.engineering. Visiting it is an ordinary web request. The host may record request data (IP address, browser, page requested) so the site stays up.

Children

Snap & Wind does not ask for personal information.

Changes

When Snap & Wind’s data handling changes, this page is updated.
