Privacy Policy — CANnery

Last updated: September 2, 2026.

CANnery is a native CAN analyzer for Mac and iPad from Wilmoth Engineering, LLC. This page covers CANnery only. It is not the privacy policy for Doodle A Day or for evengineeringlab.com.

What the app does

CANnery opens logs and traces, loads a DBC, decodes and charts signals, and includes Demo Bus so you can learn the layout on the device. Plus adds live adapters, transmit, J1939, UDS, and export.

You do not need an account to use CANnery. Demo Bus and the rest of Free run without signing in.

What stays on your device

Logs, DBC files, traces, charts, and live bus traffic you work with stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself. Demo Bus runs locally.

We do not run a CANnery account system. We do not operate a CANnery server that receives your files or bus traffic.

If you delete the app, data stored in the app goes with it. Device backups (iCloud or a local backup) may keep copies if you have those turned on. That is Apple’s backup, not a CANnery server.

Share Live Session

Share Live Session / Connect to Mac is optional. When you turn it on, CANnery advertises on your local network (Bonjour _cannery._tcp) so a Mac and iPad on the same LAN can share a live session. That link is unencrypted LAN, gated by a six-digit pairing code. USB adapters stay on the device.

Plus

Plus is an in-app purchase through the App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes the purchase and the subscription. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment card. Cancel in Settings.

This page on the web

This policy is hosted on wilmoth.engineering. Visiting it is an ordinary web request. The host may record request data (IP address, browser, page requested) so the site stays up.

Children

CANnery is a tool for working with CAN. It is not aimed at children. We do not require personal information to use the app.

Changes

If how CANnery handles data changes, we will update this page.

Contact
