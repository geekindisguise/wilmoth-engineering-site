01 — About

Two disciplines, one signal.

I'm an electrical engineer by trade and a photographer by obsession. Days go to load calculations, wiring harnesses, and firmware; the rest goes to lenses, light, and motion. Wilmoth Engineering is where those two halves actually talk to each other — EV conversions with dashboards worth looking at, consulting work that gets documented like it matters, and a growing habit of shooting the process along the way.

Based remotely, working with clients who need someone equally comfortable in a schematic and behind a camera.
