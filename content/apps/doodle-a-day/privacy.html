Privacy Policy — Doodle A Day

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

Doodles, notes, and moods stay on your device and in your private iCloud. Sync uses the CloudKit private database. Wilmoth Engineering does not receive that journal.

What stays on the device

Doodles, notes, and mood selections stay on the device and in your private iCloud. Wilmoth Engineering cannot read them. The app does not ask for an account, your location, your contacts, or your photos.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. If you allow tracking, the app can share the advertising identifier with Google AdMob and other companies for ads across their apps. The app's privacy manifest declares that identifier, and product interaction for analytics and third-party advertising, as data used to track you. If you decline tracking, the app does not use the advertising identifier for that sharing. Ads can still appear. You can turn tracking off later in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Google's ad software declares that it may also receive a coarse region (country or region), product interaction, advertising data, and diagnostics. In that software's manifest those categories are not marked as tracking. The app does not ask for location permission. These are the categories the software declares. They are not a description of any one request.

Artist, a $2.99 one-time purchase, removes ads.

Apple services

Artist purchases go through the App Store and StoreKit. The journal stays on your device and syncs through your private iCloud. Those services follow Apple's privacy policy.

Backup and sharing

You can back up every page to a file, or share a page. Sharing can send this page, a scrapbook, or a flip-book. You choose where it goes. Wilmoth Engineering does not keep a copy of a backup or a shared page.

Age rating

The App Store shows Doodle A Day's age rating as 13+.

Deleting the journal

There is no control in the app that deletes a page or the journal. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. It does not remove the private iCloud copy, and it does not remove the copy already on your other devices.

The private iCloud copy stays until you delete Doodle A Day's data from iCloud. On an iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, tap iCloud, then tap Storage or Manage Account Storage. Tap Doodle A Day, then tap Delete Data from iCloud and confirm. On a Mac, open the Apple menu, choose System Settings, click your name, click iCloud, then click Manage. Click Doodle A Day, click Delete from iCloud, then click Remove from iCloud. Apple removes that iCloud data from devices that sync with the same account. A device that has not synced yet still has its local copy until it syncs, or until you delete the app on that device.

Changes to this policy

This policy can change. Significant changes are noted in the app or in the App Store update notes.
