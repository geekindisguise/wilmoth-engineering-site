Privacy Policy — Snap & Wind

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

Snap & Wind is a disposable-film camera for iPhone from Wilmoth Engineering, LLC. This page covers Snap & Wind.

What the app does

Snap & Wind takes photos the way a disposable film camera would. There is no account and no sign-in.

Photo storage

Photos are captured and stored on the iPhone.

Backup to iCloud is optional. When that switch is on, undeveloped frames and the roll journal are copied to your own iCloud account. That storage belongs to your Apple Account, not to Snap & Wind. Wilmoth Engineering does not operate a server that receives those photos.

Photos are saved to the Photos library or shared only when you choose.

If the app is deleted, photos stored only in the app go with it. Copies saved to Photos, or already backed up to your iCloud, stay where they were put.

Optional iCloud backup — stop and remove

The in-app iCloud backup switch is optional and off unless turned on.

  • Turning the switch off stops future backups. It does not delete copies already in iCloud.
  • Wilmoth Engineering has no server copy of those files and cannot erase them from iCloud.

To remove copies already in iCloud, first turn off the in-app backup switch to stop future backups. Then use Apple’s iCloud storage controls:

  1. Open Settings, tap the Apple Account name at the top, then tap iCloud.
  2. Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage.
  3. Find Snap & Wind in the list and use Apple’s controls there to delete that app’s iCloud data.

Exact labels can vary by iOS version. Apple documents checking and managing iCloud storage, including third-party app data, at Check your iCloud storage and Manage your iCloud storage.

Camera and Photos access

Snap & Wind uses the camera only to take photos in the app. It does not use location or the microphone.

When saving a developed print, Snap & Wind asks for permission to add photos to the Photos library. It does not request permission to read the existing photo library.

Access can be changed or turned off later in iPhone Settings:

  • Camera: Open Settings, tap Privacy & Security, then Camera. Turn off Snap & Wind.
  • Photos: Open Settings, tap Privacy & Security, then Photos. Tap Snap & Wind and choose None to stop the app adding photos.

An app appears in those lists only after it has asked for that permission. Apple documents these controls at Control access to hardware features on iPhone and Control access to information in apps on iPhone.

Tracking and ads

Snap & Wind does not track people and shows no ads. There is no account system.

Analytics

There are no analytics and no third-party SDKs. The App Store privacy label for Snap & Wind is “Data Not Collected”.

Purchases

There is one optional in-app purchase, Full Kit. It is a one-time unlock. Apple handles payment entirely, and the app never sees payment details.

Children
