Privacy Policy — Doodle A Day

Last updated: October 20, 2026.

Doodle A Day keeps each doodle, note, and mood in your own private iCloud. Sync uses the CloudKit private database. Wilmoth Engineering has no access to that content.

What stays in your iCloud

Doodles and drawings, notes, and mood selections stay in your private iCloud. The app does not collect them, and Wilmoth Engineering cannot read them. The app does not ask for an account, your contacts, or your photos.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the device ID (after the App Tracking Transparency prompt), coarse location, usage data, advertising data, and diagnostics. You can limit ad tracking in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Artist, a $2.99 one-time purchase, removes ads.

Apple services

Artist purchases go through the App Store and StoreKit. Doodles, notes, and moods sync through iCloud and the CloudKit private database. Those services follow Apple's privacy policy.

Where the journal lives

Doodles, notes, and moods sync through your own private iCloud. They are not uploaded to Wilmoth Engineering servers. Deleting the app on one device does not delete the private iCloud copy. You can remove the journal from iCloud in your device's iCloud settings.

Sharing

Wilmoth Engineering does not sell, trade, or share your personal data. When you share or export a page, you choose where it goes. Wilmoth Engineering does not keep a copy of shared pages.

Children

Doodle A Day is rated 4+. The app does not require an account or personal information. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your control

The journal is in your private iCloud, so you control it. In the app you can view your pages, delete entries, and share a page. Uninstalling the app leaves the private iCloud copy in place until you remove it from iCloud.

Changes to this policy

This policy can change. Significant changes are noted in the app or in the App Store update notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@wilmoth.engineering
