Support — Doodle A Day

Last updated: October 20, 2026.

This page is how to get help with Doodle A Day, the daily doodle journal for iPhone and iPad.

How to get help

Email hello@wilmoth.engineering and name Doodle A Day in the message. Include whether the device is an iPhone or an iPad, and the iOS version.

iCloud sync

Doodles, notes, and moods sync through your private iCloud. Use the same Apple ID on the iPhone and the iPad, and turn iCloud Drive on. Wilmoth Engineering cannot read the journal.

Restore Artist

Artist is a $2.99 one-time App Store purchase on the Apple ID that bought it. On another device, sign in with that Apple ID, open the App Store account page, and download Doodle A Day from Purchased. A different Apple ID does not include the purchase.

Widgets and reminders

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show today's doodle. Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, add a widget, and choose Doodle A Day. A daily reminder uses the time set in the app. If the reminder does not appear, open Settings, then Notifications, and allow notifications for Doodle A Day.
